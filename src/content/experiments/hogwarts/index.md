## Why I built it

One of my first complete pages.

## What I learned

1. IntersectionObserver turns on a sticky navigation once the hero leaves the screen.
2. Event delegation, so one listener handles every navigation link.
3. House buttons and their content in matching arrays, so clicking one shows its own by index.
4. Form logic with if/else, checking age and trait to choose a house.

## Revisited

1. Nov 2023: moved the navigation to event delegation.
2. Aug 2024: fixed the form buttons and smoothed the scrolling.
