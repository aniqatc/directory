## Why

Not everything has to be a website. I wanted to build something that lives in the terminal.

## What it does

1. Guided prompts in five categories: growth, gratitude, mindfulness, questions and quotes.
2. Tag and mood each entry, then find entries by date, tag, prompt type or mood.
3. Streaks, word counts, writing goals and a monthly mood calendar.
4. Everything stays on your computer, so it works offline.

## What I learned

1. Commands people can remember, built with Commander and Inquirer: write, show, prompts, tags, moods, stats and config.
2. Storing entries as local files in ~/.rflect, and working out stats from them.
3. Testing with Jest, and versioning and publishing a package to npm.
