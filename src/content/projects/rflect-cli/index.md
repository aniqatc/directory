## Why

Not everything has to be a website. I wanted to build something that lives in the terminal.

## Hardest part

A CLI is hard to show off, so its landing page plays each command as a terminal session you scroll through.

## What I learned

1. Commands people can remember, built with Commander and Inquirer: write, show, prompts, tags, moods, stats and config.
2. Streaks, word counts, mood patterns and writing goals, all stored locally in ~/.rflect.
3. Testing with Jest, and versioning and publishing a package to npm.
